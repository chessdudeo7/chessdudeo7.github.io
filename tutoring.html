HOW LESSONS WORK

Meet the student
where they are.

I adapt explanations, examples, and practice to each student’s level and goals. Every session is designed to produce understanding they can use independently.

  1. 01
    Diagnose

    Find the exact concept, habit, or position creating friction.

  2. 02
    Explain

    Break it into manageable pieces with clear reasoning and examples.

  3. 03
    Practice

    Apply the idea with guided problems, drills, and immediate feedback.

  4. 04
    Transfer

    Leave with specific next steps and the confidence to continue alone.
