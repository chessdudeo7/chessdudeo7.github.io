KnightMare

Deep learning · 2025

A chess engine that learns instead of searching. A PyTorch CNN trained on 100,000+ grandmaster games encodes each position as a 12-channel tensor and outputs a move distribution — no alpha-beta anywhere. Modal’s cloud GPUs turned hours of local training into minutes, which is the only reason it shipped inside a hackathon. Illegal-move suggestions were fixed by masking the output distribution to legal moves at inference.
